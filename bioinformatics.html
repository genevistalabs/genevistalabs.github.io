Bioinformatics

Where biology meets computing to unlock information hidden within DNA, genes and genomes.

What Is Bioinformatics?

Bioinformatics is an interdisciplinary field that combines biology, computer science, mathematics and statistics to collect, store, analyse and interpret biological data.

Modern biological research can generate enormous amounts of data. For example, DNA sequencing can produce millions or billions of nucleotide sequences. Bioinformatics provides computational approaches that help researchers make sense of this information.

In simple terms: Bioinformatics helps us use computers to understand biological information.

Why Does Biology Need Computing?

Advances in DNA sequencing and other laboratory technologies have made it possible to generate biological data at an extraordinary scale.
